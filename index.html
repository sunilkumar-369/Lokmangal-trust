About Sudama Trust

"Sudama" stands for selfless friendship and care. That is the heart of everything we do.

Sudama Trust is a charitable trust run by a team of volunteers who believe nobody should go hungry, cold or alone. We work for elderly people who have no one to look after them, poor children who deserve a chance to grow and learn, and poor families who are struggling to get through the day.

Whatever help a person needs, we try to reach them: a hot meal, clothes and blankets, financial support for emergencies, medicine, or simply someone to stand by them.

Every rupee donated to the Trust goes towards helping people on the ground. Our motto says it all — "As well as team help people."

“Real service is when the hand that gives and the heart that cares work together.” — The spirit of Sudama Trust
